# The two screenshots, step by step

Both screenshots must cover the **same Sunday to Saturday week**. If the two date ranges differ,
nothing will reconcile.

## Screenshot 1: Meta Ads Manager

1. Go to **adsmanager.facebook.com** and sign in.
2. At the top, click the **Ad sets** tab (not Campaigns, not Ads).
3. Click the date range button near the top right. Choose **Custom**. Click the **Sunday** that
   starts the week, then the **Saturday** that ends it. Click **Update**.
4. Make sure these columns are visible: **Ad set name**, **Amount spent**, **Results**.
   If the campaign name is not shown, also take the screenshot from the **Campaigns** tab, or tell
   Claude the campaign name.
5. Turn off any filter that hides ad sets that were off. Ad sets that spent nothing still matter.
6. Take a screenshot of the whole table, including the date range at the top.

## Screenshot 2: GoHighLevel funnel stats

1. In GoHighLevel, go to **Sites** in the left menu, then **Funnels**.
2. Click your funnel (the one you picked during setup).
3. Click the **Stats** tab at the top.
4. Set the date range to the **same Sunday to Saturday**.
5. The screenshot must show, for the opt-in page: **Uniques** and **Opt-ins**, and for the
   application page: **Opt-ins**.
6. Take a screenshot including the date range.

## Sending them to Claude

Drag both image files into the Claude Code window, or paste them, and say which week they are for,
for example: "week of 10/11".

For a backfill, name each file with its Sunday date, for example `2026-10-11-meta.png` and
`2026-10-11-ghl.png`, put them all in one folder, and give Claude the folder path.
